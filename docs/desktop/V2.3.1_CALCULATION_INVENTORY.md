# Energy Monitor Desktop v2.3.1 calculation inventory

Formula contract: `desktop-v2.3.1`. This map records the behavior preserved in
Phase 1. It is an implementation inventory, not a proposal to correct the
formulas.

## Reusable calculation map

| Area | Domain entry point | Inputs | Outputs and semantics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month parsing | `src/domain/dates.ts` | `YYYY-MM`, `YYYY/MM`, `Mar-26` and legacy prefixes | Canonical `YYYY-MM`; invalid is `null`; numeric parser intentionally preserves the v2.3.1 prefix acceptance. |
| Month boundaries | `src/domain/dates.ts` | month key | UTC previous month/days for Energy Cost; local `Date` + fallback 30 days for UPS/Engineering Dashboard; leap years follow the selected rule. |
| UPS energy | `src/domain/energyCost.ts` | configured/default UPS groups and `loadKw` | Every group/member is required; any missing/non-finite member makes UPS energy `null`; `sum(kW) * 24 * calendarDays`. |
| Air energy | `src/domain/energyCost.ts` | current and unique previous month air meters | Required current-minus-previous deltas; `sum(delta) * 1,000,000`; missing previous month/value is `null`. Top-level legacy fields and configured meters retain their lookup precedence. |
| DC energy | `src/domain/energyCost.ts` | configured/default panel IDs, voltage, current | Required panel values; `(V * A / 200) * 220 / 1000 * 24 * days`. Missing panel/value is `null`. |
| Cost/rate/share | `src/domain/energyCost.ts` | raw building energy/cost and calculated floor energy | Rate is `cost / energy` except null, non-finite, or zero energy; floor cost is rate × floor energy; share is floor/building × 100. |
| UPS groups | `src/domain/upsGroupAggregation.ts` | configured IDs, capacity, UPS rows | Missing `loadKw`/`loadKva` is treated as zero; load % is kVA/capacity × 100; available % is clamped at zero; energy is kW × 24 × local days. |
| Srinakarin phases | `src/domain/srinakarinPower.ts` | UPS/AC phase, PPC43 panel/current snapshots, existing rows | Phase voltage/current are averages; UPS phase loads and PPC43 panels are sums; missing aggregates fall back to existing monthly row, then null. Phase keys retain source final character. |
| Engineering Dashboard | `src/domain/engineeringDashboard.ts` | selected exact-month log, previous exact month, mapping/topology | Group/detail rows, air deltas, DC conversion, totals and Energy Cost values. Missing display numerics retain the legacy zero fallback. |
| Site Comparison | `src/domain/facilityComparison.ts` | facility logs and explicit latest allowed month | Union/common month selection, calendar display range, and per-month comparison metrics; absent records remain null. Desktop adapter supplies current local month. |
| Rack Capacity | `src/domain/rackCapacity.ts` | rack status/zone records | Canonical status counts, `(blank)`/Other bucket, 0–1 ratios, locale-sorted zones, null ratios for empty denominators. |
| Rack Unit Capacity | `src/domain/rackUnitCapacity.ts` | month rows, used U, total U | Latest row strictly before month; used/total × 100, null when total is zero. |
| Chart data | `src/domain/chartData.ts` | monthly logs | Normalized sorted months and Energy Cost series; nulls are retained for chart gaps. Rendering/layout remains in React components. |
| Capacity forecast/trends | `src/utils/capacityForecast.ts`, `src/utils/trendCalculator.ts` | history points/values | Existing pure utilities remain behavior contracts and are documented for later extraction; no forecast threshold or zero-division behavior is changed in Phase 1. |
| Dashboard quality/health | `src/utils/analytics.ts`, `src/utils/capacityHealth.ts` | raw readings, Energy Cost, thresholds | Existing KPI scoring, PUE, carbon, alerts and capacity bands remain Desktop adapters; suspicious scoring is covered as technical debt rather than corrected. |

## Site-specific branches

- Rangsit uses the default UPS/DC/Air mapping and the Dashboard-FAC calculation
  path.
- Srinakarin uses explicit phase snapshots and its `UPS 41A/41B` and `PPC`
  aggregate rows. The `PPC 43` current path is a three-phase average of panel
  sums; UPS phase loads are summed.
- Workbook-provided energy calculation profiles override defaults per month.

## Source/provenance

The source of truth for this map was the v2.3.1 TypeScript implementation and
its existing regression scripts. Golden fixtures are minimized synthetic
records shaped like those inputs; they contain no copied operational workbook
values, identifiers, credentials, or workbook binaries.
